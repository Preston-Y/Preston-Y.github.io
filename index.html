Engineering Work

Interactive CAD models can be rotated, explored, and viewed directly in the browser.

01

Solar Panel Rack Systems

The model shown here is the largest of 22 solar panel rack iterations I modeled. Across the full family, the assemblies span 1–8 panels, Talesun and Meyer Burger panel types, and 6" and 8" mounting gimbals, including heavy duty (HD) versions.

The iterations share a common rack structural design while adapting to different panel counts, dimensions, and mounting requirements.

8 PanelsTalesun8" HD Gimbal

Drag the model to rotate and inspect the assembly. Additional viewer tools such as the Components menufor exploring individual parts and subassemblies are available.

02

Pump & Motor Enclosure

Pickup-bed housing enclosure designed to contain and protect a coupled pump-and-motor assembly.

The motor is currently omitted due to GitHub file-size limitations.

03

Comet LWS 3025 S

High-pressure triplex plunger pump modeled as an individual component.

This is the most detailed individual part I have modeled to date and one of the parts I am most proud of modeling.

04

Well Seal Systems

1.25" drop pipe well-seal assemblies with diameter configurations that can be explored directly in the interactive model.

This was my first time working with SolidWorks configurations, which allowed me to manage multiple product variations within the same model.

05

Carbon Motor Brush

Carbon motor brush used to transfer electrical current between stationary wiring and the rotating components of an electric motor.

I particularly enjoyed modeling this part because it required me to learn a new technique. I had never modeled a curved helix before, so I had to research how to create one.

ABOUT
MY
WORK

My CAD work ranges from individual components to complete mechanical assemblies and configurable product families. Most of the projects shown here were developed in SolidWorks and involved more than simply creating the final geometry.

Depending on the project, I worked through component selection, assembly organization, multiple design iterations, and the details needed to make each model fit within a larger system. Some projects challenged me through their complexity and number of parts, while others required me to learn new modeling techniques along the way.

I created this portfolio to show the models themselves rather than relying only on screenshots. The interactive viewers allow each assembly to be rotated, inspected, and explored at the component level. I also plan to add more of my engineering drawings and documentation as the portfolio continues to grow.
