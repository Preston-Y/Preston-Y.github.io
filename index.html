03

Comet LWS 3025 S

High-pressure triplex plunger pump modeled as an individual component.

This is the most detailed individual part I have modeled to date and one of the parts I am most proud of modeling.

04

Well Seal Systems

1.25" drop pipe well-seal assemblies with diameter configurations that can be explored directly in the interactive model.

This was my first time working with SolidWorks configurations, which allowed me to manage multiple product variations within the same model.

05

Carbon Motor Brush

Carbon motor brush used to transfer electrical current between stationary wiring and the rotating components of an electric motor.

I particularly enjoyed modeling this part because it required me to learn a new technique. Unlike many of my other models, where the challenge was mainly in time, detail, and execution, this part required me to figure out how to model a curved helix.
